A triolet triolet

Published in The Lark
To build a little triolet,
you need eight lines (first two repeat),
with one important thing to say.
To build a little triolet,
make one, three, four, five, sev’n rhyme-A.
And meter? Try ‘iambic t’.
To build a little triolet,
you need eight lines (first two repeat).
