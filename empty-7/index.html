Clouded hills and sunlit uplands

Not a triolet

Pink-faced men concerned with hedge funds
dream of pretty sunlit uplands.
Streets replete with flag-swath’d patri’ts,
shores secured, while smirking racists
guard our brown unpleasant sands,
dreaming dreams of mills satanic,
dreams of swords awake in hands,
dreams of Priti’s sunlit uplands.
