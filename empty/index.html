Empty

A tweeolet

Published on Twitter
A pane of glass, psychologic’ly
We watch the man. There’s nothing there.
Transparently untrustworthy,
A pane of glass, psychologic’ly
No values, no integrity.
No substance lies beneath that hair,
A pane of glass, psychologic’ly
We watch the man. There’s nothing there.

This poem was written during Boris Johnson's prime ministership about no one in particular.
