Hard cheese

A brieolet

Published on Twitter
I INTENDED some Lunch,
   And it turn’d to a Breakage
It began à la munch,
I intended some Lunch;
But the Brie pack’d a punch
   As I made my cheese sandwich;
I intended some Lunch;
   And it turn’d to a Breakage.
