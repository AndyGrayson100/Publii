Boys

A triolet

Published in The Lark
They grow up, the little tykes,
In the space of a heartbeat,
In the turning of a tide.
They grow up, the little tykes,
In the blinking of an eye
They’re sitting in the front seat.
They’ve grown up, the little tykes.
In the space of a heartbeat.
