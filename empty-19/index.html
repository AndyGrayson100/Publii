The bay

A triolet

Published in Lit Up
This dampened pallet, those turquoise greens
‘neath heavy skies drawn gunbarrel grey,
Waves swirling white across the sands.
This dampened pallet, those turquoise greens
cloak turmoil, seething hordes unseen
who surge relentless through the bay.
This dampened pallet. Those turquoise greens,
‘neath heavy skies drawn gunbarrel grey.
