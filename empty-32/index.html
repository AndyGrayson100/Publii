Singles

A triolet in praise of jukeboxes and 7" 45s

Published in Poetry in Form
A time machine that’s power’d by song
Encoded in scratch’d vinyl grooves.
Get back, school’s out, when we were young…*
A time machine that’s power’d by song,
God only knows when we belong.
Delights, breaks hearts, bewitches, moves.
A time machine that’s power’d by song
Encoded in scratch’d vinyl grooves.

*…at heart (a band) of gold, true, numb…
