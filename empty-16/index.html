Ragg'dy eggs

A triolet

Published in The Lark
Ragg’dy ragg’dy ragg’dy eggs
Ragg’dy poached on sou’dough toast
That’s the breakfast we love best
Ragg’dy ragg’dy ragg’dy eggs
Salt and pepper — hold the veg
That’s the eggsies we want most
Ragg’dy ragg’dy ragg’dy eggs
Ragg’dy poached on sou’dough toast
