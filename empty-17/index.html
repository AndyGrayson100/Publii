Stuck at Brough

A triolough

Published in The Lark
You rue the day y’get stuck at Brough
At least you’re not in Mexb’rough, though
Which wouldn’t have been East enough.
You rue the day y’get stuck at Brough,
Replacement bus had dropped you ough
Since driving you through Conisbrough.
You rue the day y’get stuck at Brough
At least you’re not in Mexb’rough, though
