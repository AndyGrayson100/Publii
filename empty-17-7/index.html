The Gove

A tweeolet

Published on Twitter
An inauthentic man is he
Who operates outwith the truth,
Mendacity for all to see.
An inauthentic man is he
A turn of phrase coined latterly:
To lie is now ‘to do a Gove’.
An inauthentic man is he
Who operates outwith the truth.
