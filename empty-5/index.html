Beyond

A triolet

Published in The Lark
Cold, slate-grey thing of mistery
clad mute, subdued this winter’s day.
What lies beyond that which we see,
cold, slate-grey thing of mistery?
How vast could your expanses be
outwith this safe, enshrouded bay
cold, slate-grey thing of mistery
clad mute, subdued this winter’s day?
